# User guide

## Customers

Browse Services or Find a pro, filter by category/city/locality, open a profile and review its service estimate. Register/sign in, select Request, choose one of the provider’s open dates, then choose a time for that date. Booked times are labeled **Already booked** and disabled; pending requests and provider-unavailable times are labeled separately. Availability refreshes while the booking form is open. Enter the task description and service address to send the request. View updates in Dashboard → Bookings. Pending/accepted requests can be cancelled. Completed bookings can be reviewed. Eligible bookings can be disputed through the API; a dedicated dispute form is not yet exposed in the web client.

## Providers

Use Join as a provider and complete your profile. New profiles remain pending until an administrator checks the application; document upload is evidence submission, not approval. In Dashboard, choose **Profile & services** to edit your biography, skills, experience, city/locality, service areas, categories, working hours, timezone and unavailable intervals; add or deactivate service offerings; and submit private verification documents. Customer booking times are offered only in your configured timezone during available one-hour working-hour slots. Then manage requests from Bookings: accept or decline pending requests, start accepted work, and mark work completed. Recorded earnings are completed-job amounts, not platform payments.

## Administrators

Sign in with an admin account, review pending provider applications on the dashboard and approve/reject with a recorded reason. Booking CSV export is available from that panel. More advanced account, category and dispute controls are available via protected API; a full admin management interface remains future work.

All seeded accounts are demonstration data. Never represent seeded approved providers as real verified identities.
